# DECISION LOG

> Append-only. Every meaningful decision (health, trading, money, project direction, agent permissions) gets a row.
> Agents: you may append rows with `Decided by: PENDING — needs G`. You may never edit or delete existing rows.

| Date | Area | Decision | Reasoning | Decided by | Review date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM-DD | (health/trading/work/project/agents/money) | | | G / PENDING | |

---

## How to use

- **Decision**: one sentence, concrete ("Pause RAVYN until CLARIO ships v1").
- **Reasoning**: 1–2 sentences. Enough for a future AI to understand why.
- **Review date**: when to check if the decision still holds. Blank = permanent.
- Reversals get a NEW row referencing the old one — never rewrite history.
