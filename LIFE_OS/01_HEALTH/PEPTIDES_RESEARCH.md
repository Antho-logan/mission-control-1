## Red Lines

- No compound started without physician review — no exceptions for "mild" ones.
- No stacking a new compound with another new intervention in the same window.
- No sourcing from unverifiable vendors.
- Any adverse effect → stop, log in [[HEALTH_LOG]], escalate to physician.
